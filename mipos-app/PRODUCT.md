# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small business owners (kioscos, tiendas, servicios) in Argentina and Uruguay. They use the POS daily at the counter, often on a tablet or phone. They are not technical but understand their business numbers well.

## Product Purpose

mipos is a point-of-sale and business management tool. It lets owners charge customers, manage their catalog, track sales, and configure payment method adjustments (surcharges for cards, discounts for cash). Success means the owner spends less time on admin and more time serving customers.

## Brand Personality

Confiable, directo, local. The app should feel like a tool built by people who understand Argentine commerce, not a generic SaaS export.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (cream background, rounded cards everywhere)
- Overly technical or enterprise-looking UIs
- Anything that looks like it requires onboarding to use

## Design Principles

1. The tool disappears into the task — if users notice the UI, something is wrong.
2. State is always visible — the user should never wonder if something is active or saved.
3. Numbers are first-class — financial data needs clear hierarchy and readable typography.
4. Mobile-first operations — POS is used on phones; settings on desktop. Both must work.
5. Familiar affordances — use standard patterns; don't reinvent for flavor.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Support dark mode (already implemented). Avoid relying solely on color to convey state.
